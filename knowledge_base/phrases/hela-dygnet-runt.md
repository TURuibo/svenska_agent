# hela dygnet runt

🗣️ 类型: fast-uttryck · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 3/5
🇨🇳 24小时不间断，全天候 · 🇬🇧 around the clock, 24/7
📝 字面: the whole 24-hour period around

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- `hela dygnet runt` = [[hela-dygnet]] + runt（around）= "around the clock"
- 强调不间断、循环往复，比 `hela dygnet` 更强调持续性
- 常见于服务/活动的描述：öppet hela dygnet runt = open 24/7
