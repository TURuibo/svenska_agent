# falla för

🗣️ 类型: partikelverb · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 3/5
🇨🇳 爱上；被迷住 · 🇬🇧 to fall for; to be captivated by
📝 字面: to fall for

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Used when something/someone captures your heart or fascination.
- Can apply to a person (romantic), a place, a hobby, or an idea.
- *Sen föll jag helt för det.* = Then I completely fell for it.

- 🔄 *bli kär i* — to fall in love with (romantic, stronger)
- 🔄 *bli förtjust i* — to become fond of
