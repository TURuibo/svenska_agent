# vad kan jag hjälpa dig med

🗣️ 类型: hälsningsfras · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 5/5
🇨🇳 有什么可以帮你？ · 🇬🇧 how can I help you?
📝 字面: what can I help you with

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Standard service phrase when answering calls or greeting customers.
- Note: *med* at the end is mandatory — cannot be dropped.
- Equivalent to "How can I help you?" in English.
- Used at clinics, shops, helpdesks, etc.

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- → [[grammar-modala-hjalpverb]] (*kan* = can, modal verb)
- Preposition stranding: *med* at end (Swedish allows this)

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *Kan jag hjälpa dig?* (slightly shorter/more casual)
- 🔗 [[hjälpa]] · [[kan-jag-hjälpa-dig]]
