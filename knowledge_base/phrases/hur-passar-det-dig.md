# hur passar det dig

🗣️ 类型: fråga · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 4/5
🇨🇳 你什么时候方便？那个时间适合你吗？ · 🇬🇧 does that work/suit you? when does it suit you?
📝 字面: how does it suit you

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Used to ask if a proposed time or arrangement works for someone.
- *Hur passar det dig nästa vecka?* = When does next week suit you? / When are you free next week?
- *Passar det dig på tisdag?* = Does Tuesday work for you?
- *Passa* (v.1) = to suit, to fit, to work.

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- → [[grammar-presens]] — *passar* = presens of passa

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *Passar det dig på ...?* = Does [day] work for you?
- 🔄 *Funkar det för dig?* (more colloquial)
