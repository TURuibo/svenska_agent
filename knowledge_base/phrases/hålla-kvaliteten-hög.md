# hålla kvaliteten hög

🗣️ 类型: fast-uttryck · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 3/5
🇨🇳 保持高品质 · 🇬🇧 to keep the quality high
📝 字面: to hold the quality high

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- *om vi håller kvaliteten hög* = as long as we keep the quality high (conditional clause)
- *hålla* + object + adjective = a resultative/stative construction.
- Common in business and professional contexts.
