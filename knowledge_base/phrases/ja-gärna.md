# ja gärna

🗣️ 类型: samtycke · 语域: vardagligt · 常用度: 5/5
🇨🇳 好啊，乐意；当然愿意 · 🇬🇧 yes, gladly; yes please; I'd love to
📝 字面: yes, gladly

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- A warm, enthusiastic way to agree or accept an invitation — more welcoming than a flat *ja*.
- Common response to invitations: *Ska vi gå på bio?* → *Ja gärna!*
- Can stand alone as a complete utterance.

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- *Gärna* is an adverb meaning "gladly / with pleasure".

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *Ja, gärna det!* (more emphatic) · *Absolut!* (sure!) · *Ja tack!* (yes please)
- 🔗 [[gärna]]
