# ta med

🗣️ 类型: partikelverb · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 5/5
🇨🇳 带走；携带；随身带 · 🇬🇧 to take along; to bring with you
📝 字面: take with

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Separable particle verb. The particle "med" can separate from "ta":
  - "Ta med dina kläder" = take your clothes with you
  - "Ta dina kläder med" = take your clothes (particle at end)
- Direction matters: "ta med" = bring/take along (away from a location); contrasted with "ta hit" (bring here).
- In notices: "ta med" = make sure to remove/take your belongings.

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- → [[grammar-partikelverb]] — separable particle verb
- Imperative: "Ta med dina saker!" = Take your things!

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 ta dit (take there), ta hit (bring here)
- 🔗 [[att-ta-med]] — the café/takeaway usage ("to go")

## 例句 / 对话 (Examples)

- 🇸🇪 Glöm inte att ta med alla dina kläder! — 🇨🇳 不要忘记带走您所有的衣物！
- 🇸🇪 Ta med dig paraply — det ska regna. — 🇨🇳 带上你的雨伞——会下雨的。
- 🇸🇪 Kom ihåg att ta med legitimation. — 🇨🇳 记得带上身份证。
