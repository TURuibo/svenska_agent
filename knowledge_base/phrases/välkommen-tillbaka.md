# välkommen tillbaka

🗣️ 类型: avsked · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 4/5
🇨🇳 欢迎再次光临；欢迎回来 · 🇬🇧 welcome back; please come again
📝 字面: welcome back

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Farewell phrase inviting a return visit, used in service contexts.
- *Tillbaka* = back; *välkommen* = welcome.
- Can also be used when someone actually returns: *Välkommen tillbaka! Vi har saknat dig.* = Welcome back! We've missed you.

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- *Välkommen* agrees in number: *välkommen* (singular), *välkomna* (plural)

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *Välkommen åter!* (formal, slightly old-fashioned)
- 🔄 *Hoppas vi ses snart igen!* = Hope to see you again soon!
