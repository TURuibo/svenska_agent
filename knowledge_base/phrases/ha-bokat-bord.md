# ha bokat bord

🗣️ 类型: uttryck · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 4/5
🇨🇳 预订了餐桌 · 🇬🇧 to have a table reservation; to have booked a table
📝 字面: to have booked (a) table

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Used at restaurant check-in: *Har ni bokat bord?* = Do you have a reservation?
- Answer: *Ja, vi har bokat på [name]* = Yes, we have a reservation under [name].
- *Bord* here has no article (generic/indefinite), common in fixed expressions.
