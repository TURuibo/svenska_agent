# av en slump

🗣️ 类型: fast-uttryck · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 4/5
🇨🇳 偶然地；出于巧合 · 🇬🇧 by chance; coincidentally
📝 字面: by a chance/coincidence

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Fixed expression, typically placed at the beginning or end of a clause.
- When fronted (V2 rule applies): *Av en slump hittade jag nyckeln.* (verb follows directly)
- Very common in both spoken and written Swedish.

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- → [[grammar-av-en-slump]] (fixed prepositional phrase; V2 when fronted)

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *av en händelse* — by coincidence (more formal register)
- 🔄 *råka* + infinitive — to happen to do something accidentally

## 例句 / 对话 (Examples)

- [[sent-det-var-faktiskt-av]] 🇸🇪 Det var faktiskt av en slump. 🇨🇳 其实是偶然的。
- 🇸🇪 Av en slump fick jag jobbet. 🇨🇳 我偶然得到了这份工作。
