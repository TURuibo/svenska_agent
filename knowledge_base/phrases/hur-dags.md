# hur dags

🗣️ 类型: frågeuttryck · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 4/5
🇨🇳 几点；什么时间 · 🇬🇧 at what time; what time
📝 字面: how (at what) time

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Standard question for asking the time of an event: *Hur dags ska vi ses?* = What time shall we meet?
- Slightly more colloquial than *Vilken tid?*; both are common.
- *Hur dags?* can stand alone as a follow-up question.
