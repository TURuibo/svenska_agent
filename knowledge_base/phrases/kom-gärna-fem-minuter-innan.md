# kom gärna fem minuter innan

🗣️ 类型: instruktion · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 3/5
🇨🇳 最好提前五分钟到 · 🇬🇧 please come five minutes early
📝 字面: come gladly five minutes before

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Polite instruction to arrive slightly early, common at clinics, offices, appointments.
- *Gärna* softens the imperative, making it a polite suggestion rather than a command.
- *Innan* = before (time); here means "before the appointment time".

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- → [[grammar-imperativ]] — *kom* = imperative of komma

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *Kom fem minuter tidigt.* (more direct)
- 🔗 [[gärna]] · [[komma-i-tid]]

## 例句 / 对话 (Examples)
