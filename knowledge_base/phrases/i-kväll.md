# i kväll

🗣️ 类型: tidsuttryck · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 5/5
🇨🇳 今晚 · 🇬🇧 tonight; this evening
📝 字面: in (the) evening

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Fixed time expression: *i kväll* = tonight / this evening.
- Compare: *på kvällen* = in the evening (habitual/general), *i kväll* = tonight (specific).
- Do NOT add definite ending: ~~i kvällen~~ is wrong; it's always *i kväll*.

- 🔄 *i morse* = this morning; *i dag* = today; *i natt* = tonight (nighttime)
