# ta form

🗣️ 类型: fast-uttryck · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 3/5
🇨🇳 成形；成型 · 🇬🇧 to take shape
📝 字面: to take form

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Used both literally (e.g., dough forming into bread) and figuratively (a plan taking shape).
- *Se hur brödet tar form* = watch the bread take shape.
- Also: *Projektet börjar ta form.* = The project is beginning to take shape.

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)
