# tack för besöket

🗣️ 类型: avsked · 语域: neutralt · 常用度: 4/5
🇨🇳 感谢光临 · 🇬🇧 thank you for your visit; thanks for coming
📝 字面: thank you for the visit

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Used by service staff when guests leave (restaurant, shop, clinic).
- Often paired with *välkommen tillbaka* = welcome back / please come again.
- *Besöket* = the visit (definite form of *besök*)

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- *Tack för + definit substantiv* = Thank you for [the thing] — fixed gratitude pattern

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *Välkommen åter!* = Welcome again! (slightly more formal)
