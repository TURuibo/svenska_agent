# bara du själv

🗣️ 类型: vardagsfras · 语域: vardagligt · 常用度: 3/5
🇨🇳 只需要你自己（不必带任何东西） · 🇬🇧 just yourself (nothing to bring)
📝 字面: just you yourself

## 用法说明 (Usage)

- Used to reassure a guest that they don't need to bring anything.
- Typical in response to *Ska jag ta med något?* (Should I bring anything?)
- *Själv* (myself/yourself/himself…) adds emphasis to the pronoun.

## 短语中的语法 (Grammar inside)

- *Bara* = only/just; *själv* = oneself (emphatic pronoun)

## 变体 & 相关 (Variations & Related)

- 🔄 *Ta med dig ingenting.* = Don't bring anything.
- 🔗 [[ta-med]]

## 例句 / 对话 (Examples)

- [[sent-bara-du-sjalv-vi-ses-snart]] 🇸🇪 Bara du själv! Vi ses snart. 🇨🇳 只需要带你自己！我们很快见！
- 🇸🇪 Ska jag ta med mat? — Nej, bara du själv! 🇨🇳 我需要带食物吗？——不，只需要带你自己！
