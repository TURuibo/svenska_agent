# tisdag — substantiv en

📖 中文：周二；星期二 · English: Tuesday
发音提示：TIS-dag

## 语法变形 (Forms)

| Form | Swedish |
|------|---------|
| Singular obestämd | (en) tisdag |
| Singular bestämd | tisdagen |
| Plural obestämd | tisdagar |
| Plural bestämd | tisdagarna |

Time expressions:
- *på tisdag* = on Tuesday (this coming Tuesday)
- *i tisdags* = last Tuesday
- *på tisdagar* = on Tuesdays (habitually)

## 词组搭配 (Collocations)

- `på tisdag` — 这周二
- `i tisdags` — 上周二
- `varje tisdag` — 每周二
- `tisdag eller onsdag` — 周二或周三

## 例句 (Sentences)

- 🇸🇪 Tisdag eller onsdag fungerar bra. — 🇨🇳 周二或周三都可以。
- 🇸🇪 Vi ses på tisdag. — 🇨🇳 我们周二见。
- 🇸🇪 Mötet är i tisdags. — 🇨🇳 会议是在上周二。

## 用法提示 (Usage Notes)

- Days of the week are NOT capitalized in Swedish.
- Use *på* for "on Tuesday (this week)"; use *i tisdags* for last Tuesday.
