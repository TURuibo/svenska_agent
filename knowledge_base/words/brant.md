# brant — adjektiv

发音提示：/brant/

## 语法变形 (Forms)

| form | svenska |
|------|---------|
| en-form (grundform) | brant |
| ett-form | brant |
| definit / plural | branta |

## 词组搭配 (Collocations)

- `ett brant berg` — 一座陡峭的山
- `en brant backe` — 一个陡坡
- `brant klippa` — 陡峭的岩壁
- `branta sluttningar` — 陡峭的斜坡
