## 用法提示 (Usage Notes)

- Compound: ludd (lint/fluff from fabric) + filter. Very practical household vocabulary.
- Always needs emptying after each tumble dryer use — standard instruction in Swedish laundry rooms.
- "Ludd" also means fluff on fabric generally (e.g., from a sweater).
