# toppen — adjektiv / utrop

📖 中文：太棒了；顶级的 · English: great; awesome; top
发音提示：TOPP-en

## 语法变形 (Forms)

- As exclamation: **Toppen!** — invariable (no agreement)
- As adjective (informal): *toppen* — also invariable in colloquial use
- Base noun: **topp** (en) = top, peak

## 词组搭配 (Collocations)

- `Toppen!` — 太棒了！（感叹词）
- `Det är toppen!` — 那太棒了！
- `en toppenkille / en toppendag` — compound: 很棒的人/日子（口语）

## 例句 (Sentences)

- 🇸🇪 Toppen! Vi ses klockan fem. — 🇨🇳 太好了！我们五点见。
- 🇸🇪 Det är toppen att du kan komma! — 🇨🇳 你能来太棒了！
- 🇸🇪 Det låter toppen! — 🇨🇳 听起来很棒！

## 用法提示 (Usage Notes)

- Primarily used as an enthusiastic interjection, similar to "Great!" or "Awesome!".
- More informal than *utmärkt*; widely used in spoken Swedish among all ages.
- Can also precede nouns as an adjective compound: *toppenjobb*, *toppendag*.
