# 📗 av en slump — 固定短语：偶然 (fixed phrase: by chance)

级别: B1
一句话: *Av en slump* 是"偶然地/凑巧"的固定说法，前置时遵守V2规则。

## 规则说明 (Rules)

📐 结构公式:
```
Subject + verb + av en slump          （句末）
Av en slump + verb + subject          （句首，V2）
```

与中文对比: 中文"偶然"可以自由放置；瑞典语前置时动词必须紧跟（V2）。
与英文对比: Same as "by chance" — can appear at the end or the beginning.

## 详细讲解 (Detail)

- *Av en slump* is a frozen prepositional phrase. It cannot be modified (*av ett stort slump* is wrong).
- When it begins the sentence (fronted), Swedish V2 rule requires the verb to come directly after:
  - ✅ *Av en slump fick jag jobbet.* (I got the job by chance.)
  - ❌ *Av en slump jag fick jobbet.* (wrong — subject before verb)
- Near-synonym: *av en händelse* (more formal/literary register).

## 例句 (Examples)

- ✅ [[sent-det-var-faktiskt-av]] 🇸🇪 Det var faktiskt av en slump. 🇨🇳 其实是偶然的。
- ✅ 🇸🇪 Av en slump hittade hon sin gamla dagbok. 🇨🇳 她偶然找到了她的旧日记。
- ❌ *Av en slump hon hittade sin gamla dagbok.* ← V2 saknas

## 常见错误 (Common Mistakes)

- ⚠️ Placing the subject before the verb after fronting: *Av en slump jag hittade...* (wrong)
- ⚠️ Trying to add an article or adjective: *av ett slump* (wrong — it's fixed as *en slump*)
