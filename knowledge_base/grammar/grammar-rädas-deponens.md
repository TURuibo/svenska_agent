# 📗 rädas (deponensverb) — 关身动词 rädas（害怕） (deponent verb: rädas)

级别: B2
一句话: *Rädas* 以 -s 结尾但含义为主动（害怕），永远不用不带 -s 的形式。

## 规则说明 (Rules)

📐 结构公式:
```
Subject + räds + object          (present)
Subject + räddes + object        (past)
Subject + har rätts + object     (perfect)
```

与中文对比: 中文"害怕"是普通动词；瑞典语 *rädas* 看起来像被动，但意思是主动。
与英文对比: English "to fear" is a normal verb; Swedish *rädas* is a deponent — -s ending is permanent.

## 详细讲解 (Detail)

**Deponent verbs** (*deponensverb*) are verbs that end in -s in all their forms but are **active in meaning** — they do NOT have a passive sense.

| Tense | Form |
|-------|------|
| Infinitiv | rädas |
| Presens | räds |
| Preteritum | räddes |
| Perfekt | har rätts |

- *räds* = is afraid of (present)
- *den som inte räds tidiga mornar* = the person who is not afraid of early mornings
- NEVER use *rädar* (this form does not exist)

Other common deponent verbs: *minnas* (to remember), *hoppas* (to hope), *trivas* (to thrive), *hälsas* (to be greeted).

## 例句 (Examples)

- ✅ [[sent-det-ar-ett-yrke]] 🇸🇪 Det är ett yrke för den som inte räds tidiga mornar. 🇨🇳 这是一个不怕早起的人的职业。
- ✅ 🇸🇪 Hon räds inte utmaningar. 🇨🇳 她不害怕挑战。
- ❌ *Hon rädar inte utmaningar.* ← Deponentverb tar alltid -s

## 常见错误 (Common Mistakes)

- ⚠️ Using *rädar* instead of *räds* — deponent verbs always keep the -s.
- ⚠️ Confusing with passive: *brödet räddes* would be passive of a non-existent *räda*, not deponent *rädas*.
